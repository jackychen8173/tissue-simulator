Model: Growth and division mechanics only — full PIN+PD canalization model NOT included
Tissue: Semicircular leaf (exact dimensions unknown)
Run time: t=100
Reactions: MoveVertexRadially, WallGrowth::Stress, VertexFromWallSpring, Division::ShortestPath2D

Isolated test of the growth and division system before integrating it with auxin
dynamics. Vertices move radially outward at a uniform rate (MoveVertexRadially),
walls grow irreversibly under stress (WallGrowth::Stress), and cells divide when
they exceed a volume threshold (Division::ShortestPath2D). No auxin, PIN, or
plasmodesmata reactions present. Goal was to confirm that the tissue grows and
divides stably before adding chemical complexity.

Changes from previous: First run with growth/division. No auxin model — purely mechanical.
