Model: Holloway et al. 2025 (Front. Plant Sci. 16:1621815) PIN+PD auxin canalization
Tissue: Small semicircular leaf (exact dimensions unknown — model file not saved)
Run time: Unknown
T_pin: Low (below paper value)

One of four runs from this date that swept across different PIN transport strengths to
see how the strength of active PIN-mediated auxin transport affects the canalization
pattern. With low T_pin, PIN transport is weak and auxin moves mostly by passive
diffusion. Expected result: little or no polarized PIN allocation, weak or absent
vein-like Dij strands.
