Model: Holloway et al. 2025 full PIN+PD canalization model with anisotropic growth
Tissue: 10x50 semicircle, 500 -> 748 cells by t=200
Run time: t=200
T_pin: 3.0
V_threshold: 1.5
k_growth_x: 0.002 (lateral/x growth rate)
k_growth_y: 0.006 (apical/y growth rate)

Anisotropic growth implemented by replacing the single MoveVertexRadially reaction
with two separate reactions: GrowthForce::X and GrowthForce::Y, each with its own
rate. With k_y = 3x k_x, the tissue extends 3x faster in the vertical (apical)
direction than sideways, producing a taller, narrower, pointier leaf shape compared
to the isotropic runs. Division pattern and cell count are similar to the previous
run (500->748 cells). Dij still shows uniform decay (same T_pin=3.0 issue).

Changes from previous: Growth replaced from isotropic (single k) to anisotropic
(separate k_x, k_y). Tissue shape is now elongated in y. Reaction count: 14->15.
