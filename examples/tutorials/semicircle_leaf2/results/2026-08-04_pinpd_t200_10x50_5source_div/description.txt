Model: Holloway et al. 2025 full PIN+PD canalization model with working growth and division
Tissue: 10x50 semicircle, 500 -> 748 cells by t=200
Run time: t=200
T_pin: 3.0
V_threshold: 1.5

Critical bug fix: the division crash was caused by numWallTmp (used in the topology
assertion after divideCell) being measured AFTER divideCell instead of BEFORE.
This made the assertion 'numWallTmp + 3 == T->numWall()' always false, crashing
every division attempt regardless of cell geometry. Moving numWallTmp to before
the divideCell call fixed all crashes.

With V_threshold=1.5 restored, cells divide at ~2.5x their initial size. The outer
ring cells (ring 9) try to divide first but were excluded because they are boundary
cells (elongated arc slices). Interior cells divide in waves roughly every 20 time
units, growing the tissue from 500 to 748 cells by t=200. Dij still decays
uniformly to 0.2 — strand formation still not achieved at T_pin=3.0.
