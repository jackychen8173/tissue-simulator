Same as full_pinpd_model_t112_6x20 but with Dij forced to be symmetric — both
sides of each wall (Dij and its mirror) kept equal throughout. In the standard
model, the two Dij values on a wall can in principle diverge. This run tests
whether enforcing symmetry changes the pattern, or whether the strands still form.
